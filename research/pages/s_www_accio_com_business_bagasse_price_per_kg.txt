- **Evidence:**
  - Vietnam import prices reached $730–$750/MT ($0.73–$0.75/kg) in late 2025 [Price-Watch](https://www.price-watch.ai/bagasse-pulp-prices/).
  - China FOB prices averaged $660–$680/MT ($0.66–$0.68/kg) [Price-Watch](https://www.price-watch.ai/bagasse-pulp-prices/).
  - Global market size for bagasse pulp and paper is anticipated to reach $2.53 Billion by 2026 [Marketreportsworld](https://www.marketreportsworld.com/market-reports/bagasse-pulp-and-paper-market-14721624).

#### Raw Sugarcane Bagasse Fiber (Feedstock)

- **Snapshot:** Unprocessed or minimally processed fibrous residue directly from sugar mills.
- **Trend Status:** Mature/Low Value. Primarily used for cattle feed or as fuel for sugar mill boilers.
- **Evidence:**
  - Market price in India for 20kg blocks is approximately ₹3.50/kg (approx. $0.042/kg) Indiamart.
  - Supply is highly seasonal, depending on the sugarcane crushing season (Q4 to Q1 in many regions) [Price-Watch](https://www.price-watch.ai/bagasse-pulp-prices/).
- **Risks:** High volume/low value makes logistics the primary cost driver; susceptible to moisture-related degradation if not stored properly [Businessresearchinsights](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/bagasse-products-market-119591).
- **Verdict:** **Cautious** (low profit margins unless vertically integrated).
